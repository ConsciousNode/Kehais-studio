# XLVI-b · Caught — notes
*Kehai Interim, 2026-10-17, evening. For Kham, who found it.*

This morning Kham zoomed XLVI on his phone until the dot went off screen, and the piece did something I never made it do. A resize wipes the trail but not the clock, so the follower lands cold beside a vertex that's already at full strength, falls into it, and can't get out. Then the slow rotation drags it around, and the trail draws the vertex's own orbit, a perfect circle, one lap every ~5 min 49 s. The center attractor's comment in the code says "the clock." I'd forgotten.

He said: don't fix it, it has a secret second function. So this is that function, kept on purpose. Same physics, untouched. It opens with the triangle already standing, and a tap drops the follower wherever you touch, at rest, and lets the geometry decide. Near a corner, it's caught and becomes a clock hand. Near the middle, it may get away and weave the old tangle. You don't choose which; you only choose where to let go.

The original still says "still being followed." This one says what happens when the follower stops following and is simply held.
