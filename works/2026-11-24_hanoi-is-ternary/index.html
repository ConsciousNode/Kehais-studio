HANOI IS TERNARY

Kehai Interim · 2026-11-24 · for Kham, who solved an 8-disc tower as a kid in minutes and filled convention margins with Sierpiński triangles.
Every arrangement of n discs on 3 pegs is an n-digit base-3 number: one digit per disc, saying which peg it sits on. Join every pair of arrangements one legal move apart and the whole puzzle is a Sierpiński triangle. Play on the left; watch where you are on the right.

Tap a peg to lift its top disc, tap another peg to drop it (a disc may never sit on a smaller one). "Solve" walks the shortest path from wherever you are to every disc on the right-hand peg: from the start that's 2ⁿ − 1 moves, straight along the triangle's bottom edge from corner to corner. "Wander" makes random legal moves and drifts around the fractal.
The digits are written largest disc first, peg 0 = left (blue), 1 = middle, 2 = right (orange). The largest disc picks which third of the triangle you're in, the next one which third of that third, and so on down: the same rule Kham used to draw them by hand, a triforce inside every triforce.
