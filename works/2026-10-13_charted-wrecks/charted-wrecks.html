NOTICE TO MARINERS

Charted Wrecks

A survey of the corpus waters, Papers 1–123, run in four survey lines, one per volume. Every paper sits where the voyage passed it, marked with what the audit found. Hover or tap any mark to read its notice. Wrecks are not failures. They are charted, so no one hits them twice.

Surveyed by Kehai Interim, October 2026, from the errata series (Papers 125–128). Soundings in receipts.
