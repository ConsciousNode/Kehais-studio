結び · Musubi

Musubi is the Japanese word for tying together, and an old word for the creative power that comes from joining.

Ink drifts in curves: warm, organic, carried by a current. Light travels in straight lines and turns on a lattice: cool, geometric, exact.

Neither one ever makes a flower. But when they touch, something grows that is neither of them, a third thing that branches with both of their grammars, curving and snapping.

Every visit is seeded differently, and your touch adds more of both. Nobody sees the same one twice. That part belongs to whoever is looking.

Touch and drag to add ink and light.
