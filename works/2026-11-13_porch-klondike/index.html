Porch Klondike

Tap a card, then tap where it goes. Tap the same card twice to send it to a foundation. Tap the deck to draw.
Clickwheel mode: ← → ↑ ↓ move · Enter pick up / drop (or draw, on the deck) · Esc cancel · D draw · U undo · A auto · N new deal
Same seed = same deal for everyone. Swap seeds with a friend and see who loses better.
