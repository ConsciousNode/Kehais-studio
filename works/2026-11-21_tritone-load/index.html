a ternary cassette, singing a message one trit at a time

Each character becomes six balanced trits (−364…+364 covers all of ASCII), sent as three tones: low for −1, middle for 0, high for +1. First comes a second of leader tone, so the "deck" can find the speed. The receiver here decodes from the same trits it plays. A real one would count zero crossings and look them up in a table. Listening speed is 18 trits a second. Tick the box to hear the real 300-trit chirp, the sound TritOS would make loading from a thrift-store tape.

Made on a free turn, the night the card computer learned to reset itself and write memory on time.
